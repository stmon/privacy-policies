Domov privacy policy

Domov by panjlab · last updated 13 October 2026

Domov shows how long until each member of your family is home. It is built to share as little as possible: your family sees when you will be home, not where you are, unless you choose to share your location.

Who holds your data

Each family uses its own free Google Firebase project, set up by the person who starts the family, in their own Google account. panjlab does not run a server, does not receive your data and cannot read it. The family's admin and Google (as Firebase's provider) are the ones who hold it.

What stays on your phone

  • Your exact location, used to work out your travel times.
  • Your places (home, work, school and others) and the times you arrive at and leave them.
  • Your settings.

What your family sees

  • The name you choose.
  • Your state: at home, away, on the way (with the arrival time and the destination's name), or paused.
  • Your travel time to each of your places you share, and whether you are at one of them.
  • How far you are from home, rounded to a kilometre.
  • Only while "Share my location" is on: your location rounded to about 100 metres. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Family.

Sign-in

You sign in with your Google account (your name and email address go to your family's Firebase project), or join without an account, on that phone only.

Travel times

To get a travel time with live traffic, your phone sends the start and end point, rounded to about 10 metres, to the map service your family chose (TomTom or Mapbox), using the family's own key. Nothing else is sent with it.

Background location and activity

With your permission, Domov uses your location while it is closed, so your family's times stay right when you leave or arrive, and during a trip. With the optional "Physical activity" permission, it tells walking from driving. Both stay on your phone; only the results above are shared.

No ads, no tracking, no selling

Domov has no ads and no analytics, and nobody's data is sold or given to anyone else.

Deleting your data

In Domov: Settings → Family → Delete my account deletes your data in the family and your account. Leaving the family deletes your data in it. Data kept only on your phone is removed when you uninstall Domov. The person who started the family deletes everything by deleting the family's Firebase project. More on the account deletion page.

Children

Domov is meant for families; a parent or guardian sets it up and invites the others.
