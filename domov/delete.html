Delete your Domov account

Domov by panjlab

In the app

  1. Open Domov → Settings (the gear) → Family.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This deletes your data in your family (your name, state, travel times, and your rounded location if you shared it) and your account in your family's Firebase project. Your places and settings stay on your phone until you uninstall Domov.

Delete some data, keep your account

  • Your location: Settings → Family → turn off Share my location. Your rounded location is removed from the family at once.
  • Everything in the family: Settings → Family → Leave the family deletes your data in it (name, state, travel times, location). Your account stays.
  • A place: in the place's sheet, turn off Share with family; its times and visits are no longer shared.
  • Data on your phone (places, arrival and departure times, settings): remove a place in Settings, or uninstall Domov.

Nothing is kept after deletion: there are no backups or retention periods on our side.

If you started the family

Your family lives in your own Firebase project. To delete everything for everyone, open the Firebase console → your project → Project settings → Delete project.

Without the app

Ask your family's admin to remove you, or write to info@patpad.eu and we will tell you how. panjlab itself holds no data about you.
