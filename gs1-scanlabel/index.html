Privacy Policy — GS1 ScanLabel

Last updated: March 23, 2026

Overview

GS1 ScanLabel ("the App") is a barcode scanning and label creation tool. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is handled.

Data Collection

GS1 ScanLabel does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The App operates entirely offline and does not communicate with any external servers.

Device Permissions

  • Camera — Used solely to scan GS1 DataMatrix barcodes. Camera frames are processed on-device using ML Kit barcode detection. No images or video are stored, transmitted, or shared.
  • Microphone (Audio) — Required by the underlying camera framework (CommunityToolkit.Maui Camera2 API). The App does not record, store, or transmit any audio.
  • Vibration — Used for haptic feedback when a barcode is successfully scanned.
  • Storage / Media access — Used to read images from your device for barcode scanning from saved photos. The App does not modify or delete any files on your device.

Data Storage

All data created within the App (scanned barcode history, label templates, settings) is stored locally on your device in a private application database. This data is not accessible to other apps and is not transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google ML Kit for on-device barcode recognition. ML Kit processes all data locally on your device. The barcode model may be downloaded automatically by Google Play Services. See Google's ML Kit privacy information for details.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any data from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this same URL with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the developer contact information listed on our Google Play Store listing.
